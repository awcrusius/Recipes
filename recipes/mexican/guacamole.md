## Ingredients

- 3 ripe avocados
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
- 1/2 medium white onion, finely diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely minced
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 medium tomato, seeds removed, diced

## Instructions

1. Halve and pit the avocados. Scoop the flesh into a medium bowl.
   - 3 ripe avocados
2. Add the lime juice and salt, then mash to your preferred texture — leave it chunky or make it smoother.
   - 1 lime, juiced
   - 1/2 tsp fine salt
3. Fold in the onion, jalapeño, cilantro, garlic, and tomato. Taste and adjust salt and lime as needed.
   - 1/2 medium white onion, finely diced
   - 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely minced
   - 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, finely chopped
   - 1 clove garlic, minced
   - 1/2 medium tomato, seeds removed, diced
4. Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface to limit browning and refrigerate until ready to serve. Best eaten the same day.

## Notes

- The key to guacamole is ripe avocados — they should yield to gentle pressure but not feel mushy.
- For extra heat, leave some jalapeño seeds in.
