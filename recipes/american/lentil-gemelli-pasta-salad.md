## Ingredients

- 1 1/3 cups gemelli pasta
- 1 can brown lentils (398 mL), drained and rinsed
- 110 g cherry tomatoes
- 1 avocado
- 60 g red onion, finely diced
- 50 g arugula
- 8 g fresh basil
- 8 g fresh dill
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp cashew butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar, to taste
- 2 tbsp basil puree (for dressing blend)
- 1 tbsp vegan mayonnaise (for dressing blend)
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard (for dressing blend)
- 3/4 tsp sugar (for dressing blend)
- 1/4 tsp roasted garlic powder (for dressing blend)
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. **Cook the pasta** — Bring a large pot with 2.5 L of salted water to a boil. Add the gemelli and cook for 10–13 minutes, or until al dente. Drain through a sieve and rinse under cold water until chilled.
   - 1 1/3 cups gemelli pasta
2. **Prepare the ingredients** — Wash the tomatoes, basil, and dill. Cut the lemon in half. Cut the tomatoes in half. Pick and chop the basil and dill leaves. Cut the avocado in half, remove the pit, and scoop out the flesh with a spoon. Drain and rinse the lentils in a sieve.
   - 110 g cherry tomatoes
   - 8 g fresh basil
   - 8 g fresh dill
   - 1 lemon
   - 1 avocado
   - 1 can brown lentils (398 mL)
3. **Make the dressing blend** — In a small bowl, stir together the basil puree, vegan mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, sugar, and roasted garlic powder until smooth.
   - 2 tbsp basil puree
   - 1 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
   - 2 tsp Dijon mustard
   - 3/4 tsp sugar
   - 1/4 tsp roasted garlic powder
4. **Prepare the dressing** — In a large bowl, mash the avocado with a fork. Whisk in the olive oil, dressing blend, and cashew butter, then add apple cider vinegar and half of the lemon juice to taste.
   - 2 tbsp olive oil
   - 2 tbsp cashew butter
   - 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar, to taste
5. **Combine** — Add the lentils, tomatoes, onion, dill, and basil to the dressing. Mix well.
   - 60 g red onion, finely diced
6. **Assemble the pasta salad** — Add the drained pasta to the bowl, then gently stir in the arugula and season to taste with salt and pepper.
   - 50 g arugula
   - salt and pepper, to taste
7. **Serve** — Divide the pasta salad into large serving bowls. If desired, squeeze the remaining lemon juice over top. Enjoy!

## Notes

- If the avocado isn't ripe enough to mash, slice it instead and use it as a garnish when serving.
- The salad is served chilled, so it keeps well — a good make-ahead lunch.
